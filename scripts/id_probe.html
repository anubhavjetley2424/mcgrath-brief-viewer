Welcome to Sutherland Shire Council Community Profile

The Sutherland Shire Estimated Resident Population for 2025 is 241,327, with a population density of 723.3 persons per square km.

Sutherland Shire is located at the southern coastal border of the Sydney metropolitan area, about 26 kilometres from the Sydney CBD.

Sutherland Shire Council Community Profile provides demographic analysis for the Shire and smaller areas within it based on results from the 2021, 2016, 2011, 2006, 2001, 1996 and 1991 Censuses of Population and Housing. The profile is updated with population estimates when the Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS) releases new figures.
